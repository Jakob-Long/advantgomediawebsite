Common questions

What does a Facebook and Instagram ads agency do?

We create and run your ads on Facebook and Instagram, build the website or landing pages those ads send people to, and set up tracking so you can see which ads bring in customers. For service businesses, we also build the system that puts appointments and quotes on your calendar.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local service businesses of every kind, like roofing, restoration, auto repair, HVAC, and other trades, and stores of every kind: online stores, clothing stores, and local shops. Whatever you sell, the goal is the same: more customers at a cost that makes sense.

What is the booking system?

It is the system behind your ads. We build the website or landing page, then a booking flow that puts appointments or quotes straight onto your calendar. Leads are routed to you automatically, so you are not chasing them.

Do you work with businesses outside Portland, Maine?

Yes. We are based in South Portland and work with local businesses across Southern Maine. Online businesses can work with us from anywhere, since the work is done online.

How much does it cost?

Your ad budget goes to Meta and is separate from our fee. We go over pricing on your growth audit call, and there are no long-term contracts.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on your offer, your budget, and your tracking. We start with an audit, launch with controlled budgets, and test quickly so you can see what is working early. We do not promise a specific number before we have looked at your account.

What happens on the growth audit call?

In 20 minutes we look at your current ads, tracking, and website, point out where money is leaking, and map out next steps. If you are not running ads yet, we talk through what a first campaign would look like.

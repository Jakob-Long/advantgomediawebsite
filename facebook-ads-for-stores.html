Common questions

What kinds of stores do you work with?

Online stores, clothing stores, local shops, and any business that sells products. Online stores can work with us from anywhere. For local stores in the Portland area, we can meet in person.

Can ads bring people into my local store?

Yes. Ads can be limited to shoppers near your store and point them to an offer, a page, or a call. We track calls and online orders back to the ad, and can use offer codes to measure in-store response.

What is return on ad spend (ROAS)?

It is the revenue your ads bring in for every $1 you spend on them. A 3x return on ad spend means $3 in revenue for every $1 in ad spend. It is revenue, not profit, so we also watch cost per customer.

What size stores do you work with?

We work best with founder-led stores doing roughly $15,000 to $100,000 a month that want to scale profitably.

Do you handle the ad creative and website pages?

We write the angles and creative briefs, test them quickly, and build mobile-first pages that match each ad.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your ad account, store, and tracking, plus a short call about your products and goals. The audit shows what needs fixing before we spend more.
